We look forward to gathering and practicing together in this lovely new space! Below is our official weekly schedule starting late July:

### Weekly Schedule

* **Saturday Teachings (Core Session):** 17:00 – 19:30 *(Starting July 25th)*  
  The main centerpiece of our teachings.  
  *Suggested Donation:* 40 zł
* **Wednesday Review & Practice:** 18:00 – 20:30 *(Starting July 29th)*  
  A time to review materials and ask questions of each other (Lama Dorje will attend upon request).  
  *Suggested Donation:* 20 zł
* **Sunday Social Events:** Usually around 15:00  
  Solely for our enjoyment and community! We are looking for volunteers to step up and organize things like hikes, casual meals, city tours, or picnics. Please reach out if you can lead one!

### Food, Refreshments & Guests
Bringing any food or drinks to share is always greatly appreciated! Please feel free to bring a friend as well—everyone is warmly invited.

### Center Fundraising & Accessibility
We are actively fundraising to establish and furnish a permanent center so we have a practice home even when Lama Dorje isn't here. If you are able to contribute more, your extra support will help cover scholarships and support this vision. 

**Our doors are open to everyone:** If you find this financial contribution difficult, please know you are fully supported and warmly welcomed to attend regardless.
