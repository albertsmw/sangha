We would like to invite you to a meeting that will take place this Sunday at 3PM in Jordan's Park. The idea behind it is to gather together as a new community wishing to share the Buddhist path together, get to know each other, share food, ask questions and enjoy a beautiful afternoon in Kraków. The meeting will start with a short introduction to the Four Immeasurables followed by a meditation session, upon request. Feel welcome to bring picnic blankets and something to eat or drink!

The meeting will be held in English with Polish translation.

**Date:** Sunday, July 12, 2026  
**Time:** 3:00 PM – 6:15 PM  
**Location:** Park Jordana, Kraków
