We would like to invite you to our next Sangha gathering! The main goal of this meeting is to build a community on our Buddhist path and create an open space for conversation and connection. 

We will start with a teaching from Lama Dorje followed by a Q&A session to kick off our discussion. Afterwards, we'll try a simple and fun movement exercise inspired by physical theatre, pantomime, and dance improvisation to share some joy. We will then keep the space open to enjoy food, talk, and get to know each other better.

Feel welcome to bring picnic blankets to sit comfortably and something to eat or drink to share with the group!

The meeting will be held in English with Polish translation.

**Date:** Sunday, July 19, 2026  
**Time:** 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM  
**Location:** Park Jordana, Kraków
